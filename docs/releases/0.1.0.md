Budget Pro's first 0.1 release is a personal budget desktop app that keeps your budget files on your device.

### Features

- Organize your budget into editable groups and categories.
- Plan monthly income and category amounts, with income, planned, spent, and remaining totals at the top.
- Add, edit, search, and delete expense transactions.
- View spending breakdowns and your monthly spending pace.
- Create, open, save, and save copies of `.budget.json` files using native dialogs.
- Automatically reopen the last budget, with validation, atomic saves, and protection for unsaved changes.

### Downloads

Choose the archive for your computer, extract it, and launch the application inside:

- **macOS Apple Silicon:** `budget-pro-darwin-arm64.tar.gz`
- **macOS Intel:** `budget-pro-darwin-x64.tar.gz`
- **Windows x64:** `budget-pro-win32-x64.zip`
- **Linux x64:** `budget-pro-linux-x64.tar.gz`

`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for the four archives. These are unsigned application bundles; keep their supporting files together. macOS and Windows may show security prompts for an unsigned download.

### Current limitations

- USD budgets and an English interface.
- Plans and transactions are independent for each month, with no automatic rollover.
- Budget files are ordinary, unencrypted JSON files.
- Updates are manual: save your budget, close the app, and replace the application with the newer download. Keep your budget files outside the application folder.
